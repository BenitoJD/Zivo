# Zivo

**[zivo.fyi](https://zivo.fyi)**

Zivo is a global intelligence company. We collect public information from across the world — corporate records, jobs, sanctions, maritime activity, legal filings, procurement, media, finance, and more — and turn it into intelligence you can trust and use.

Most data companies pick one topic and build a product around it. We build one permanent record of the public world: what happened, who was involved, and the proof behind it. That record grows over time and works across every country and domain we add.

Our advantage is not software anyone can copy overnight. It is the archive itself — years of captured evidence, connected facts, and history that does not get thrown away when something changes.

We publish that intelligence as dossiers, timelines, monitors, and data products for anyone who needs to understand how the world is moving — researchers, journalists, investors, governments, and businesses.

We are building for the long term: a global intelligence company that should still matter decades from now.
